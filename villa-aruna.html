The residence

A sanctuary where traditional Balinese craft meets the ease of contemporary tropical living.

Villa Aruna is arranged as a sequence of open pavilions around a private garden and infinity pool. Deep roof lines temper the tropical sun, while sliding glass walls let every room open naturally to the landscape.

The villa is designed for unhurried stays: quiet mornings by the water, long lunches in the open dining pavilion, and evenings framed by timber, stone, and soft garden light.

Architecture & craft

Made with patience.

Reclaimed teak, hand-cut volcanic stone, woven rattan, and traditional joinery give the villa its tactile character. Each material was selected to age gracefully in Bali's tropical climate.
